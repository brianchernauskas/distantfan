Where to watch Buffalo Bills games in Portland

4 bar and fan watch spots in the Portland area where Bills fans watch the game, including 3 official fan club and alumni chapters. Last checked October 2026. Next game: Bills at Rams, Monday, October 12 on ESPN.

Looking for a Bills bar in Portland? Bills Backers in the area watch games at Billy Blues Bar and Grill in Vancouver, Clubhouse Bar & Grill and The Cheerful Tortoise, plus 1 more below. Listed groups include Bills Backers of the Couve, PUBS- Portland United Bills Supporters and Portland Buffalo Bills Fans. Call ahead on game days to make sure the bar will have your game on.

  • Billy Blues Bar and Grill

    Bills Backers of the Couve
    7115 NE Hazel Dell Ave, Vancouver, WA 98665

    Official Bills Backers chapter (Bills Backers of the Couve) gameday home. Bills fans gather here to watch every game.

    5134 SW Beaverton-Hillsdale Hwy, Portland, OR 97221

    Official Bills Backers chapter (PUBS- Portland United Bills Supporters) gameday home. Bills fans gather here to watch every game.

    Checked September 2026 · Source: Buffalo Bills official Bills Backers chapter map (yinzcam)

    Portland Buffalo Bills Fans

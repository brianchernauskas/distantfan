Looking for a Steelers bar in Denver? Steelers Nation in the area, from Westminster and Lakewood, watch games at Bender's Sports Bar and Grill in Westminster, Society Sports and Spirits and The Rusty Bucket in Lakewood. Call ahead on game days to make sure the bar will have your game on.

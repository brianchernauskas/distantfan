Where to watch Pittsburgh Steelers games in Denver

3 bar and fan watch spots in the Denver area where Steelers fans watch the game, including 2 official fan club and alumni chapters. Last checked October 2026.

Looking for a Steelers bar in Denver? Steelers Nation in the area, from Westminster and Lakewood, watch games at Bender's Sports Bar and Grill in Westminster, Society Sports and Spirits and The Rusty Bucket in Lakewood. Call ahead on game days to make sure the bar will have your game on.

  • Bender's Sports Bar and Grill

    Steeler Nation of North Denver
    10710 Westminster Blvd, Westminster, CO 80020

    Official Steelers bar and official Nebraska Huskers watch site, 35 HD screens and 32 beers on tap, next to the ice rink.

    Checked October 2026 · Source: Bender's Bar & Grill website

  • Society Sports and Spirits

    Bolt Fam Bars Denver
    1434 Blake St, Denver, CO 80202

    LoDo near Ball Arena, official alumni bar for the Chargers, Texans, Steelers, Iowa State and Arkansas. Known for its Tailgate setup.

    Checked October 2026 · Source: Society Sports and Spirits website

  • The Rusty Bucket

    3355 S Wadsworth Blvd, Lakewood, CO 80227

    Self-described most popular Steelers bar in the Denver metro. Shows every Steelers game, including the 7am Oct 25 kickoff from Paris.

    Checked October 2026 · Source: The Rusty Bucket website

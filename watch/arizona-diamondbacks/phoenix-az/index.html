Looking for a Diamondbacks bar in Phoenix? Diamondbacks fans in the area watch games at Cold Beers & Cheeseburgers (Chase Field), Crown Public House and Tom's Watch Bar Phoenix. Call ahead on game days to make sure the bar will have your game on.

  • Cold Beers & Cheeseburgers (Chase Field)

    401 E Jefferson St, Phoenix, AZ 85004

    Sports bar inside Chase Field overlooking left field, open daily during baseball season. No game ticket needed via the Jefferson St entrance.

    Checked September 2026 · Source: Cold Beers & Cheeseburgers

  • Crown Public House

    333 E Jefferson St, Phoenix, AZ 85004

    Downtown pub across from Chase Field that bills itself as a pre and post-game spot for D-backs fans, with TVs throughout the bar.

    Checked September 2026 · Source: Crown Public House

  • Tom's Watch Bar Phoenix

    3 S 2nd St Suite 117, Phoenix, AZ 85004

    Downtown Phoenix sports bar steps from Chase Field/Footprint Center; wall-to-wall screens for D-backs, Suns, Cowboys and MLB Extra Innings games.

    Checked September 2026 · Source: Tom's Watch Bar Phoenix

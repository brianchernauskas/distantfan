How it works

Three steps to game day

Takes about a minute. You only share a rough area, never your address.

Pick your teams

Choose up to eight across the NFL, FBS college football, NBA, major-conference college basketball, MLB, NHL and MLS. Follow the one you grew up with and the one you married into.

Set your home base

Pick your metro or use your location, which we round off to a 3-mile area before it leaves your phone.

See how many fans of your team live around you, where they watch, and who's heading out for the next game.

What's inside

A live map of where your team's fans live, shown by area, never as exact pins. Find out you're one of 200 Packers fans in Tampa.

Watch spots

Bars and backer clubs that put your team's game on. Fans add them, and you can see how many people plan to be there for the next game.

Game-day rooms

A chat for your team nationwide, and one just for fans in your area. Plan a watch party or just yell about the refs.

Your schedule

Every team you follow in one list, in your time zone, with the TV network, so you know whether to find a bar or stay on the couch.

Privacy

We never show where you live

A fan map is only useful if people feel safe on it. Here's how that works.

  • Area, not addressYour location is rounded to a grid square about 3 miles wide on your own device. The exact spot is never sent or stored.
  • Enforced by the databaseThe server rejects anything more precise than that grid square, so no bug or bad actor can save a street-level location.
  • First name and initialOther fans see the name you choose and your teams. That's it.
  • Delete anytimeRemoving your profile takes you off the map immediately.
